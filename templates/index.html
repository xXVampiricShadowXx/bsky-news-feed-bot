AUTOMATION NOTES

Built for new stories

  • Turning on a feed marks its current stories as the starting point; they won’t all post at once.
  • Stories found while auto-posting is paused are skipped when you resume.
  • A normalized article link can only be posted once across all connected feeds, including links with common tracking tags.
  • Feed blurbs and story photos are ignored. The publisher logo is attached instead.
  • Very long headlines are left unposted rather than shortened.
